Delicious Spaghetti Recipe

This simple spaghetti recipe is perfect for a quick and tasty dinner. It only takes 20 minutes to make and is full of flavor!

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil leaves

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot and add a pinch of salt.
  2. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  3. In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté the garlic.
  4. Add tomato sauce, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Drain the spaghetti and mix it with the sauce.
  6. Garnish with fresh basil and serve hot.

Enjoy your delicious homemade spaghetti! 🍝
