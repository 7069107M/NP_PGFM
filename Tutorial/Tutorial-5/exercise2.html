Simple Spaghetti Recipe

Your quick and tasty pasta dinner in under 30 minutes!

About This Recipe

This spaghetti recipe is great for beginners and busy cooks. It’s flavorful, quick, and requires only a few basic ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add a pinch of salt.
  2. Cook the spaghetti according to package directions.
  3. In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add tomato sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Drain spaghetti and mix with the sauce.
  6. Serve hot with grated parmesan cheese on top.

Recipe Information

Detail Information
