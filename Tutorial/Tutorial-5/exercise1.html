Simple Spaghetti Recipe

This easy spaghetti recipe is perfect for a quick weeknight dinner. It takes less than 30 minutes and uses simple ingredients you probably already have at home!

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add a pinch of salt.
  2. Cook the spaghetti according to the package directions.
  3. In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add tomato sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Drain spaghetti and mix with the sauce.
  6. Serve hot with grated parmesan cheese on top.

Enjoy your delicious homemade spaghetti!
